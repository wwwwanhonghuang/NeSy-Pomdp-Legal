We employ a traditional doctrinal legal method (Rechtsdogmatik), specifically 
focusing on the 'Teleologische Auslegung' (teleological interpretation) of 
liability statutes. By analyzing the legislative intent behind the Swiss 
Civil Code (ZGB) and the Code of Obligations (OR), we establish a 
systematic basis for strict liability. However, our analysis is currently 
limited to private law and does not extend to criminal responsibility 
under the StGB.
